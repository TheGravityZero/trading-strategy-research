# Weekly pivot limit — 5% entry, permanent -25% stop

## Difference from the previous experiment

The breakeven move after five minutes is disabled. Stop-loss remains 25%
adverse from entry for the entire position.

Other rules are unchanged:

- entry 5% beyond confirmed weekly pivot;
- four-hour limit lifetime;
- take-profit at pivot;
- seven-day maximum holding time;
- test is not used.

## Result

- Setups: 17.
- Fills: 2.
- Research fills: 0.
- Validation fills: 2.

| Symbol | Exit | Holding | Gross | Net |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| AVAXUSDT | take-profit | 96m | +5.26% | +5.12% |
| BTCUSDT | seven-day time exit | 10,080m | -9.23% | -9.37% |

Mean net return: **-2.13%**. Neither position reached the permanent -25% stop.

Removing breakeven allowed the AVAX trade to recover and hit its pivot, but it
also left the BTC position exposed for seven days. With no research fills,
the result remains statistically unevaluable.
